    CicloBeef Studio offers cultural artworks and printed studio editions prepared under a quote-based, made-to-order model. Studio editions may be produced through external printing and fulfillment providers after the customer confirms the order.

    Artwork previews, mockups, colors, scale and presentation may vary reasonably between screens, materials, sizes and printing processes. This does not limit your rights when a studio edition is defective, damaged, incomplete or otherwise covered by mandatory consumer protections.

    3. Quotes, Orders and Payments¶

    Artwork requests are started through Instagram DM or Facebook Page DM. LinkedIn may be used for contracts, professional coordination and related administrative matters. Prices are provided through personalized quotes. A quote may depend on the artwork, studio edition, size, orientation, quantity, destination, shipping conditions, applicable taxes and other order details.

    The validity period of each quote will be the period stated in that quote. If no valid quote is accepted, CicloBeef Studio may need to prepare an updated quote before the order can continue.

    An order is confirmed when the customer accepts the quote and completes payment through the PayPal payment link sent with the final total for that order. Production begins only after payment confirmation and confirmation of the required order details.

    CicloBeef Studio does not provide an automated public checkout. The payment link is sent after the quote is approved and reflects the confirmed total communicated to the customer.

This does not remove any mandatory rights that apply under the law.

    6. Cancellations, Changes and Returns¶

    Because studio editions are produced according to the customer's selected artwork, studio edition, size, orientation and destination details, cancellation or change requests should be made as soon as possible.

    If production has not started, CicloBeef Studio will review whether the order can be cancelled or changed. If production has started, cancellation or change options may be limited by the made-to-order process and external provider status.
