# CicloBeef Studio Basic Landing — Value Proposition

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the official value proposition for the CicloBeef Studio Basic Landing Page.

It is used to guide landing copy, commercial structure, CTA design, visual hierarchy, and future CoreFlux Studio portfolio presentation.

## 2. CoreFlux Studio Context

This landing belongs to:

- Pack: Pack 1 — Commercial Landing Pages.
- Level: Basic Landing Page.
- Project: CicloBeef Studio Basic Landing.
- Business goal: create the first portfolio-ready Basic Landing demo for CoreFlux Studio.

## 3. Official Value Proposition

CicloBeef Studio creates premium cultural wall art for collectors, offices, homes, and curated spaces that want historical meaning, visual impact, and conversation-starting artwork.

## 4. Short Positioning Statement

Premium cultural wall art for spaces that demand more than decoration.

## 5. Hero Direction

Recommended hero title:

Premium Wall Art That Speaks

Recommended subtitle:

High-end cultural prints for collectors, offices, and spaces that demand more than decoration.

Recommended primary CTA:

See Our Collections

Recommended secondary CTA:

Request Your Quote

## 6. What CicloBeef Studio Sells

CicloBeef Studio sells premium cultural artwork as wall art and print-based visual pieces.

The offer should be communicated through curated product lines such as:

- Historical Memory Prints.
- Political Reflection Artwork.
- Social Commentary Collections.
- Custom Cultural Pieces.

These names may evolve, but the landing must clearly show that CicloBeef Studio sells artwork, not only ideas or abstract branding.

## 7. Target Customer

The Basic Landing should speak to:

1. Collectors interested in cultural or historical artwork.
2. Offices that want meaningful and premium wall decoration.
3. Homes that want conversation-starting visual pieces.
4. Cultural spaces and studios.
5. Buyers who value history, society, politics, and visual storytelling.
6. International customers who are willing to request a quote before buying.

## 8. Customer Desire

The customer does not only want decoration.

The customer wants:

- Meaning.
- Visual impact.
- Cultural identity.
- Historical depth.
- A premium object.
- A piece that can start conversations.
- A stronger emotional and intellectual relationship with their space.

## 9. Brand Tone

The landing tone should be:

- Premium.
- Cultural.
- Direct.
- Editorial.
- Serious.
- Modern.
- International.
- Clear.

The landing should avoid:

- Generic decoration language.
- Excessive poetry without commercial clarity.
- Overpromising automation.
- Overcomplicated technical language.
- Too many product options.
- Unverified price promises.

## 10. Product Formats for Basic Landing

The Basic Landing should present a limited, clear product structure.

Recommended public-facing formats:

1. Essential Cultural Print — 30 x 40 cm.
2. Signature Cultural Print — 40 x 60 cm.
3. Collector Cultural Print — 60 x 80 cm.
4. Gallery Quote — 70 x 100 cm, available by custom quote.

Important:
Prices should not be published yet in the Basic Landing.

Reason:
Final pricing depends on print size, material, finish, frame option, destination country, shipping cost, provider availability, exchange rate, taxes, payment fees, and business margin.

## 11. Manual Quote Model

The Basic Landing should explain that the customer can request a personalized quote.

Recommended customer process:

1. Choose a collection or artwork style.
2. Select a preferred size or format.
3. Send an email or social media message.
4. Receive a personalized quote.
5. Approve the quote.
6. Receive manual payment instructions.
7. The artwork is produced and shipped through a professional fine art fulfillment workflow.

## 12. Payment Positioning

The Basic Landing must not promise automated checkout.

Recommended wording:

Payments are processed after a personalized quote has been approved. CicloBeef Studio may provide manual payment instructions depending on the customer's location and the available payment method.

Possible future manual payment methods may include:

- PayPal.
- Stripe Payment Links.
- Wise.
- Bank transfer.
- Other region-specific payment methods.

These should not be described as an automated integrated checkout until the payment flow is actually implemented.

## 13. Provider Positioning

The landing may mention professional fine art fulfillment partners in a general way.

The landing should not yet describe ThePrintSpace API, automatic order creation, automated shipping calculation, or automated tracking as active Basic Landing features.

ThePrintSpace replacement of WhiteWall should be documented internally, but the Basic Landing should only communicate the customer-facing result:

- Premium print production.
- Professional fulfillment.
- Quote-based ordering.
- Size and finish availability subject to quote confirmation.

## 14. What the Landing Sells Now

The Basic Landing sells:

- A premium artwork concept.
- Curated cultural print collections.
- Manual quote requests.
- Direct contact with CicloBeef Studio.
- A professional first commercial experience.

## 15. What the Landing Does Not Sell Yet

The Basic Landing does not sell:

- Automated checkout.
- Dynamic quote calculation.
- Live inventory.
- API-based fulfillment.
- Customer accounts.
- CRM-managed orders.
- Chatbot-guided purchases.
- Automatic payment links.
- Automatic shipping calculation.
- Real-time provider integration.

## 16. Commercial CTA Strategy

Primary CTA:

See Our Collections

Secondary CTA:

Request Your Quote

Final CTA:

Start Your Request

Recommended final CTA copy:

Email us with your preferred collection, size, framing option, and destination country. We will send you a personalized quote before production.

## 17. Commercial Objective

The Basic Landing must convert a visitor into a commercial conversation.

The goal is not automatic sale yet.

The goal is:

Visitor -> understands offer -> sees product direction -> understands process -> requests quote.

## 18. CoreFlux Studio Portfolio Use

Once complete, this landing can be used as:

CoreFlux Studio — Pack 1 Basic Landing Demo.

It demonstrates that CoreFlux Studio can build a static, premium, responsive, conversion-oriented landing page for a real business idea before adding leads, chatbot, automation, APIs, or backend systems.
